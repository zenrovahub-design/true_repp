How Google Determines Local Search Ranking

Google evaluates three critical review signals when choosing which businesses appear in top local map and web search results:

  • Review Velocity: Consistent weekly reviews signal an active, thriving business far more effectively than sporadic review bursts followed by silence.
  • Average Star Rating: Profiles maintaining an average score above 4.7 stars capture the vast majority of search clicks and customer calls.
  • Review Count & Freshness: Having more verified recent ratings than nearby competitors elevates your brand authority in discovery searches.
